## 2. Corrected Face Correspondence (fixes EXP-036 Finding B)

`v0.4.7/exp037_edge_location_and_adjacency.js`'s `matchFaces()` scores every (C00 face,
feature-model face) candidate pair by **centroid distance** (not orientation label, not raw
vertex-overlap fraction — see the methodological note below) and greedily assigns the
closest-centroid pairs, refusing to match when the top two candidates for a face are within
0.5mm of each other (flagged `AMBIGUOUS` instead).

**Methodological note — why not vertex-overlap fraction, the more "obvious" fix**: an initial
version scored correspondence by fraction of literally-coincident vertices (the same primitive
already used for adjacency, §3). This produced a tied 0.5/0.5 ambiguous score between the
*true* corresponding face (e.g. C03's trimmed +Y face) and an *unrelated* face (C00's -X
face), because on a cube every corner is shared by three faces — a fillet-trimmed face's
untrimmed corners are, by construction, also corners of a different, unmodified face.
Centroid distance does not have this failure mode: a 1mm feature shifts a face's centroid by
~2×10⁻⁴m, roughly an order of magnitude less than the ≥5×10⁻³m centroid separation between any
two distinct faces of this 10mm cube. This is recorded as a concrete instance of the task's
warning that "face correspondence must account for the possibility of multiple faces sharing
the same [property]" — the failure generalizes beyond orientation labels to any single
coincident-geometry signal on symmetric shapes.

### Corrected added-face census

| Model | C00 faces | Model faces | Diff | Added (this algorithm) | Removed | Ambiguous | Ground truth (independent) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C03 (fillet) | 6 | 7 | +1 | **1** — face 6 | 0 | 0 | +1 (EXP-027) |
| C09 (chamfer) | 6 | 7 | +1 | **1** — face 6 | 0 | 0 | +1 (EXP-027) |
| C04 (hole 5mm) | 6 | 7 | +1 | **1** — face 6 (cylindrical) | 0 | 0 | +1 cylindrical face (EXP-027; independently, `CORPUS_AUDIT.json` shows C04 faceCount=7) |
| C10 (shell 1mm) | 6 | 11 | +5 | **5** — faces 6–10 (inner walls) | 0 | 0 | +5 inner walls (EXP-035, by direct per-face inspection) |

All four counts reproduce the ground truth established independently in EXP-027/EXP-035 (by
different methods: raw face counts and hand-verified per-face token comparison, respectively).
This directly reverses EXP-036's `computeTokenDiff()` result of `added: 0` for both the hole
and shell models. See `v0.4.7/EXP037_RESULTS.json` for the full per-pair matched/removed/added
listing, including every matched pair's centroid distance and vertex-overlap (reported, not
used for matching).

**Classification**: Verified (as an algorithm correction reproducing independent ground truth
— not a new claim about SLDPRT semantics).

## 3. Corrected Adjacency (fixes EXP-033/audit Finding A)

`computeAdjacency()` counts vertices of one face that have a coordinate-matching (≤10⁻⁵m)
vertex in another face; ≥2 shared vertices along a face boundary indicates a shared edge —
genuine topological adjacency, computed from the actual tessellated vertex positions archived
for this corpus, not inferred from orientation labels.

Adjacency is computed **within the modified model** (e.g. C03's own 7 faces against each
other), not against C00's original 6 faces — comparing against C00 is not meaningful here
because C00 does not contain the fillet/chamfer geometry at all (confirmed: the new face has
zero shared vertices with any C00 face, exactly as expected, since C00 predates the feature).

| Model | New face | Real within-model adjacency (shared vertices) |
|---|---|---|
| C03 (fillet) | face 6 (ec=13, vc=16, the cylindrical fillet surface) | face 4 (+Z, 8 shared), face 5 (-Z, 8 shared), face 2 (+X, 3 shared), face 3 (+Y, 2 shared) |
| C09 (chamfer) | face 6 (ec=4, vc=4, the flat chamfer surface) | face 2 (+X, 2 shared), face 3 (+Y, 2 shared), face 4 (+Z, 2 shared), face 5 (-Z, 2 shared) |
| C04 (hole) | face 6 (ec=70, vc=70, cylindrical hole wall) | face 4 (+Z, 35 shared), face 5 (-Z, 35 shared) — **only** the two directly-modified faces |

In both fillet and chamfer, faces 4 and 5 (formerly +Z/-Z, now multi-loop/pentagon,
directly modified — ec/vc/secCount/b1Len all changed) are adjacent, as expected. Faces 2 and 3
(+X/+Y) are **also** genuinely adjacent — they share an edge with the new feature face — despite
having unchanged ec/vc/secCount/b1Len. Faces 0 and 1 (-X/-Y) are **not** in the adjacency list
for either model.

For the hole (C04), the new cylindrical face's adjacency reaches only the two directly-
modified faces, not the four untouched planar faces — consistent with EXP-033/035's finding
that holes do not change tokens on structurally-unrelated faces.

**Classification**: Verified (direct computation from archived vertex coordinates).

## 4. Cross-Check: Real Adjacency vs. Token-Signature Change Set (single edge only)

Translated the within-model adjacency (§3) back to C00 face indices via the corrected
correspondence table (§2), and independently re-verified which C00→C03/C09 face pairs have a
changed Block1 token array (direct `JSON.stringify` comparison of the full token arrays in
`EXP033_FEATURE_STATE.json`, not re-derived from any assumption):

| Model | Real-adjacent C00 faces | Token-CHANGED C00 faces | Token-UNCHANGED C00 faces | Sets exactly equal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C03 (fillet) | {2, 3, 4, 5} | {2, 3, 4, 5} | {0, 1} | **YES** |
| C09 (chamfer) | {2, 3, 4, 5} | {2, 3, 4, 5} | {0, 1} | **YES** |

For both fillet and chamfer, on the one edge this corpus tests, the set of faces genuinely
topologically adjacent to the new feature face (real vertex-sharing, not orientation) is
**exactly** the set of faces whose Block1 token signature changed — this includes +X/+Y
(adjacent, not directly modified, token changed) and correctly excludes -X/-Y (not adjacent,
token unchanged).

**Interpretation, stated precisely to avoid overclaiming**: this is observation, not causal
proof. It shows that for this one edge and these two feature types, adjacency (real,
geometrically computed) and token change coincide exactly. It does **not** show that adjacency
*causes* the token change (a third factor correlated with both is not ruled out), and it says
**nothing** about whether a fillet/chamfer at a *different* edge would move the effect — that
is precisely NQ-028's untested question (§6).

**Classification**: Strong Evidence (single edge, correlational, not causal) that adjacency-to-
new-feature-face and token-signature-change coincide for fillet/chamfer. This upgrades the
prior status ("Unknown — EXP-033's adjacency test was invalid," per the 2026-08-14 audit
Finding A / FH-031 correction) for the *specific, narrower* claim "the +X/+Y faces that change
in C03/C09 are genuinely adjacent to the new feature face" — which is now directly measured,
not assumed "by construction." It does **not** resolve FH-031/OQ-032/OQ-033's broader
"adjacency causes signature changes" question, which remains Unknown pending §6, and it does
not resolve NQ-027/NQ-028.

## 5. Contrast Case: Shell (not part of NQ-028, checked because the corrected tooling made it directly testable)

The same real within-model adjacency test applied to C10 (shell) shows its five new inner-wall
faces (indices 6–10) ARE, by shared-vertex count, adjacent to several of C10's five unmodified
outer walls (e.g. inner wall face 6 shares 2 vertices with outer walls at C10 indices 7 and 9,
and with the modified opening face 0). Yet EXP-035 independently established, by direct
per-face token comparison, that all five of C10's original outer walls keep **identical**
tokens to C00 (`originalOuterFaceComparison.allIdentical: true` in `EXP035_RESULTS.json`).

**This means real geometric adjacency to a newly added face is not, by itself, a sufficient
condition for a token-signature change across the feature types tested in this corpus.** The
§4 fillet/chamfer result is evidence for a correlation that holds for those two feature types
on the one tested edge — it is not evidence for a universal "adjacency always causes a token
change" rule, and should not be cited as one. Any future explanation of the fillet/chamfer
effect needs to also explain why shell's structurally-analogous new-face adjacency does not
produce the same effect.

**Classification**: Verified observation (shell outer-wall tokens unchanged, independently
established by EXP-035; adjacency-without-change is a directly measured contrast here, not an
inference).

## 6. New Model Required (NQ-028's actual discriminating experiment — NOT executed)

Full specification, to whichever session generates it:

- **Base geometry**: identical to `C00_cube_10mm` (same 10mm cube, same origin, same units,
  same SolidWorks version/export pipeline as the rest of the controlled corpus).
- **Feature type**: a single fillet or single chamfer (either suffices to answer NQ-028;
  running both, as C03/C09 did on the first edge, is optional bonus replication, not required).
- **Feature parameter**: identical magnitude to the existing model — 1mm — to avoid
  confounding location with size.
- **Feature location (the one deliberate variable)**: a **different** vertical edge than the
  one used by C03/C09 (which modify the edge shared by +X and +Y, per §2–§4 above). The
  cleanest choice is the edge shared by -X and -Y (the diagonally opposite vertical edge),
  since it changes only which two faces border the feature while preserving the rest of the
  cube's symmetry. A horizontal edge (e.g. shared by +X and +Z) is a reasonable secondary
  follow-up (tests whether the effect is vertical-edge-specific) but is not required for the
  minimal discriminating test.
- **Must remain identical / controlled**: cube size, base sketch, feature type, feature
  parameter, SolidWorks version, and the export triplet (`model.SLDPRT`/`model.STEP`/
  `model.STL`) under `test files original/controlled/<name>/`, matching the directory layout
  every existing v0.4.7 script (e.g. `vertex_analysis.js`) already expects. No other feature,
  edge treatment, or scale/unit change should be introduced — a single isolated variable
  (edge location), per the task's "do not create arbitrary variations" constraint.
- **Suggested name**: `C12_cube_fillet_1mm_edge2` (or `_chamfer_1mm_edge2`).

**Once generated**, re-run `v0.4.7/exp037_edge_location_and_adjacency.js`'s `analyzePair`
against a new `C00_cube_10mm_vs_C12_...` entry (built the same way `vertex_analysis.js` built
the existing entries — parse both models with `parser/v0.1/src/parser-core.js`, extract full
per-face vertex arrays). No new adjacency or correspondence logic is needed; both are already
generic to any (baseline, feature-model) pair.

**Predictions (restated from the audit's original NQ-028 proposal, unchanged by this
experiment)**:
- Changed-token faces move to the two faces bordering the *new* edge (e.g. -X/-Y instead of
  +X/+Y) → supports Hypothesis A (spatial/topological); promotes §4's "strong evidence, one
  edge" adjacency claim to a general, re-testable causal candidate; justifies retiring
  `isAdjacentToModified()` in `exp033_feature_state.js` in favor of `computeAdjacency()` here.
- Changed-token faces remain +X/+Y regardless of the new edge → **falsifies** Hypothesis A as
  tested here; supports Hypothesis B (feature-type/global-serialization-state) or an
  unidentified confound (e.g. "first edge-feature in the tree," independent of location).

## 7. Final Report

### What was tested

Whether the existing controlled corpus could answer NQ-028 (does moving a fillet/chamfer to a
different cube edge move the affected Block1 token faces, discriminating spatial/adjacency
causation from global/feature-type causation). Finding: it cannot (§0). Instead, tested and
corrected the two measurement tools flagged by the 2026-08-14 archivist audit as prerequisites
for NQ-028 — a genuine vertex/edge-sharing adjacency test and an orientation-independent face
correspondence algorithm (§2–§3) — and used them to directly re-measure, from real vertex
coordinates, whether the faces already known to change token signatures under fillet/chamfer
(+X/+Y, from EXP-032/033) are genuinely topologically adjacent to the new feature face, for
the single edge location this corpus contains (§4). Also checked hole (§3) and shell (§5) as
contrast cases, since the corrected tooling made this directly checkable with already-archived
data.

### What changed

Nothing about the SLDPRT format or corpus. What changed is measurement quality: (a) the
corrected face-correspondence algorithm now automatically detects added faces for hole and
shell (previously silently dropped to 0 by EXP-036's orientation-bucket bug — §2); (b) the
corrected adjacency test now directly confirms, from real vertex-sharing rather than an
assumption, that the C03/C09 +X/+Y faces are genuinely adjacent to the new feature face while
-X/-Y are not (§3–§4).

### What remained invariant

The underlying facts already established in EXP-032/033/035 did not change: fillet and
chamfer still change +X/+Y token signatures with ec/vc/secCount/b1Len unchanged; -X/-Y remain
untouched; hole and shell still do not change tokens on structurally-unrelated faces; shell's
outer walls remain token-identical to C00 despite being geometrically adjacent to shell's new
inner walls. This experiment re-verified these facts by an independent method; it did not
overturn any of them.

### Spatial/topological interpretation

For the single edge tested, real geometric adjacency (not orientation) and token-signature
change coincide exactly for both fillet and chamfer (§4) — consistent with, and now measured
support for, the spatial/adjacency explanation (Hypothesis A) *as applied to this one edge*.
However, the shell contrast (§5) shows adjacency-to-a-new-face is not universally sufficient
for a token change across feature types, so this is not evidence for a general "adjacency
causes change" law — only a correlation confirmed for fillet/chamfer here. Whether the
spatial explanation holds when the edge itself is moved (NQ-028's actual question) is
untested.

### Global-state interpretation

Not supported or refuted by this experiment. Hypothesis B (the same +X/+Y faces change
whenever any edge-type feature is applied, regardless of location, e.g. due to a
monotonically-incrementing internal ID or first-edge-feature-in-tree effect) remains
observationally indistinguishable from Hypothesis A with only one edge location tested: face
index/serialization order and physical adjacency are perfectly confounded in this corpus
(both C03 and C09 happen to modify the edge whose two faces are also indices 2/3). Nothing in
this experiment moves the needle on Hypothesis B specifically.

### Hypotheses strengthened

- "The +X/+Y faces that change token signature in C03/C09 are genuinely topologically
  adjacent to the new feature face" — upgraded from an unmeasured "by construction" assumption
  (per the audit's Finding A) to directly measured, from real vertex coordinates (§3–§4).
- "Fillet/chamfer, hole, and shell all add at least one new face" (audit Finding B) — now
  independently reproduced by a second, geometry-based method with zero discrepancies against
  the original ground truth (§2), rather than resting only on hand-verification.

### Hypotheses weakened/falsified

None newly falsified. Note: "adjacency causes token-signature changes," taken as a *universal*
rule across all feature types, is weakened by the shell contrast (§5) — real adjacency exists
there without a token change — even though the narrower fillet/chamfer-specific correlation
(§4) is strengthened. This is a refinement of scope, not a falsification of either the
fillet/chamfer finding or of FH-031's corrected Unknown status.

### Remaining unknowns

1. NQ-028's core question: does the effect move with the edge? Untested; needs the model in §6.
2. Why adjacency (where it applies) produces a token change — a correlation is now measured
   for fillet/chamfer on one edge (§4); no mechanism is established.
3. Why shell's adjacent-but-unaffected outer walls behave differently from fillet/chamfer's
   adjacent-and-affected +X/+Y faces (§5) — an open, currently unexplained discrepancy.
4. Everything already listed as unknown in `knowledge/RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md` "Current Unknowns"
   (Block1/Block2 token semantics, `[4,8,2,N]` header meaning, etc.) — unaffected by this
   experiment.

### Highest-information next experiment

Generate the model specified in §6 (`C12_cube_fillet_1mm_edge2` or equivalent — a 1mm
fillet/chamfer on the edge shared by -X and -Y, all else identical to C00/C03/C09) and re-run
`v0.4.7/exp037_edge_location_and_adjacency.js` against it. This is still the single highest
information-gain experiment for NQ-027/NQ-028: it directly discriminates Hypothesis A from
Hypothesis B with one new model, and the corrected adjacency/correspondence tooling needed to
analyze it (§2–§3) is already built, tested, and reused with no changes required. Not
automatically begun.
