## Key Conclusions

1. **Block1 tokens are COMPLETELY INVARIANT** under geometric transformations (2× scaling and translation)
2. **Block2 is COMPLETELY INVARIANT** under geometric transformations
3. **Only vertex coordinates change** under geometric transformations
4. **Strong evidence:** Block1/Block2 structures are independent of the tested absolute vertex coordinates
5. **All invariants PASS** across all transformations
6. **Unknown:** Exact semantic meaning of Block1/Block2 tokens
7. **Not established:** That Block1/Block2 specifically encode topology
