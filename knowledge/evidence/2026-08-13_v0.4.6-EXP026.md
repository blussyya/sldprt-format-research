## Method

For each of the 1,172 faces validated by the corrected pipeline (valid `[4,8,2,N]` Block1 header, valid `[4,8,2,M]` Block2 header at the corrected offset), the script independently re-derives `secCount` (= M, Block2 body length) and checks for an alternative `[4,8,2,N']` header at the two established fixed positions (`mp-20` for N'=1, `mp-24` for N'=2 — same detection window as EXP-021/023, not extended). It then evaluates four counterexample conditions per face:

1. `secCount === 1` and NOT (has alternative with N=1)
2. `secCount === 2` and NOT (has alternative with N=2)
3. `secCount >= 3` and HAS an alternative (any N)
4. Has an alternative AND `altN !== secCount`

## Result

**No counterexamples in any of the 4 tested directions, across all 1,172 faces and all 7 files individually.** This reproduces v0.4.5's cross-tabulation with a second, independently-written extraction pass built specifically to search for exceptions.

Per task instructions, this is recorded as an **empirical correlation with zero known counterexamples on the tested corpus**, not a proven causal or structural law, and not a semantic finding about what `secCount` or the alternative header represent. See `knowledge/KNOWN_INVARIANTS.md` INV-019 (Status: Correlation) and `knowledge/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` OQ-018 (updated, causality left unresolved).
