## Finding

The two scalar arrays are present on all 42 tag-4006, 33 tag-4007 and 311 tag-4009 faces. They occur on only 4 of 47 tag-4003 faces and on no tag-4001/4002/4005 faces. Presence is therefore not a universal consequence of the candidate surface type alone.

On the four scalar-bearing tag-4003 faces (all from the distributor model), array 0 behaves as an angle in radians and array 1 as negative axial distance in metres from parameter slots 0–2. Maximum axial discrepancy is 2.2812612922846263e-10 m. All four angular tests pass the preregistered-in-source 1e-7 m positional threshold; swapped-array and reversed-order angular controls pass on zero faces, as does the positive axial sign control.

The angular test anchors its phase at the first non-apex vertex and tests both orientation signs against every other non-apex vertex. The source comment says "first two" but the implementation uses one anchor, not two. Its radius comes from observed coordinates, so the angular test alone does not independently prove the cone-radius equation or reconstruct positions without geometry. No absolute angular reference basis is decoded.

Candidate cone equation: with normalized stored direction a, origin o=params[0:3], axial coordinate h=dot(p-o,a), radial distance rho, radius r=params[6], angle alpha=params[7], test rho=abs(r-h*tan(alpha)). This passes on 27 of 47 faces at 1e-7 m. Opposite-slope and constant-radius alternatives each pass on zero faces. All 20 failures are Pocket Wheel faces, with residuals much greater than the threshold. Preserve these failures; do not loosen the threshold to declare universal success. Slots 3–5 are only recorded as a candidate apex, not independently established here.
