## Key Conclusions

1. **C10 IS parseable** under the validated extraction model (11 faces, all pass INV-016/017/018)
2. **EXP-033 failure was a tooling/filtering issue**, not a structural parsing failure
3. **Shell does NOT change tokens on existing outer faces** (5/5 IDENTICAL to C00)
4. **Shell adds 5 new inner wall faces** with unique token signatures
5. **Shell modifies the +Z face** from ec=4/vc=4 to ec=10/vc=10 (multi-loop)
6. **New inner face tokens are NOT found in C00** — they are shell-specific signatures

## Relationship to Previous Experiments

- **EXP-033**: C10 was reported as "no parseable faces". EXP-035 determines this was a filtering issue.
- **EXP-034**: Geometric transformations do NOT change tokens. EXP-035 shows shell also does NOT change tokens on existing faces.
- **EXP-033 (fillet/chamfer)**: Fillet/chamfer cause token changes on unrelated faces. Shell does NOT. This suggests fillet/chamfer are unique in producing global token changes.
