## Method and reproducibility

Run from repository root:

```sh
node v0.4.8/exp052_multiview.js
python v0.4.8/exp052_sheets.py
```

Node uses built-in modules and the actual parser/v0.2 triangle indices. Python requires Pillow and DejaVuSans.ttf. The renderer is derived from EXP-051, using a shared material and bounding box for each pair, identical orthographic cameras, and no boundary overlays. Original coordinates/triangle connectivity are not repaired, welded or projected. Six axis views plus opposite upper/lower isometric views are rendered at 360 × 360 pixels. All 13 controlled models and both USB halves have original STL comparisons (120 paired views); Pocket Wheel and Dekor add 16 parser-only views. Committed sheets cover eight representative models; the scripts regenerate all individual tiles.

STL coordinates are converted from mm to metres. USB exports require a fixed translation of **(-33.5, 0, -20.5) mm**: their original bounds start at zero while the SLDPRT is centered in X/Z. Dimensions agree before translation; no rotation or scale fitting is performed. Raw/translated bounds, input SHA-256 hashes, parser diagnostics, triangle counts and per-view metrics are in [EXP052_RESULTS.json](../../v0.4.8/EXP052_RESULTS.json).

Difference panels: grey means common coverage and depth difference ≤0.01 mm; orange means common coverage with larger depth difference; magenta is parser-only coverage; cyan is STL-only coverage. The 0.01 mm threshold is a visualization flag, not an acceptance tolerance. Depth compares the nearest visible surface along each camera ray.

## Results

| Model | Lowest silhouette intersection/union over eight views | Largest per-view depth 95th percentile |
|---|---:|---:|
| C10 hollow cube | 100% | <0.000001 mm |
| C04 through-hole | 99.9296% | 0.002515 mm |
| C07 two holes | 99.8039% | 0.006714 mm |
| USB TOP | 99.9781% | 0.013492 mm |
| USB BOTTOM | 99.9782% | 0.010589 mm |

The hollow cube agrees in silhouette and depth, including its cavity and reverse view. USB TOP preserves the cutouts, bosses, bores and enclosure walls from both sides. Remaining orange areas concentrate around curved features and edges; different display/STL tessellations are consistent with this pattern, but this is not a proof that every discrepancy is harmless. The parser has 4,704 triangles versus 7,534 in the TOP STL, and 1,488 versus 2,486 in BOTTOM.

**Reference defects matter:** C03, C09 and C11 show large depth discrepancies on the -X side, consistent with EXP-049's missing reference face finding. C03's reverse isometric view visibly exposes the missing STL wall. Its silhouette overlap still exceeds 99.99%, demonstrating why silhouette alone cannot validate a solid. Maximum ray-depth discrepancies near a curved hole can also be large when one tessellation exposes a distant surface and the other covers it; those maxima are retained in JSON and must not be described as point-to-surface errors.

Pocket Wheel preserves its central bore/keyway and pocketed teeth in the inspected eight views. Dekor preserves the fretwork from above/below and thin profile from the sides. **Neither has an independently verified reference in this comparison.** The historical attachment contains similarly named STL files in `test files converted`, alongside parser-generated exports; their provenance was not established, so they were not treated as ground truth.

## Review qualifications

- EXP-051's claim of “no inverted or missing faces” is stronger than its evidence. Its renderer (and this comparison renderer) is double-sided and uses absolute normal lighting, so inverted winding can look correct. One angle also cannot expose hidden omissions. No winding/watertightness conclusion is made here.
- EXP-050's chord results support the interpolation explanation. They do not independently prove that all 47 cone metadata records are correct; independent STEP agreement remains limited to the eight USB-bottom records previously checked. One-sided residuals alone cannot exclude every systematic error.
- Pixel comparisons are resolution-dependent, sample only visible surfaces, and are not Hausdorff distances or exact CAD/B-rep validation. Small details can fall below one pixel. No universal accuracy tolerance, new invariant or parser fix is claimed.
- The original workspace had an altered Pocket Wheel file; this experiment used the committed staging snapshot in a separate worktree, preserving the altered file.
