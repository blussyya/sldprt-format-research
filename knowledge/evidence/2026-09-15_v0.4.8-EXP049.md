**Date produced/captured:** 2026-09-15. **Status:** Replication plus one new corrective finding. No new invariant proposed; nothing promoted.

Source script: [`v0.4.8/exp049_independent_replication.js`](../../v0.4.8/exp049_independent_replication.js).

Raw output: [`v0.4.8/EXP049_RESULTS.json`](../../v0.4.8/EXP049_RESULTS.json) (per-file counts and SHA-256 values).
Inputs: all `.sldprt` under `test files original` (24; 21 decoded, 3 legacy OLE2 unsupported) and the 13 controlled `model.STL` exports.

## 1. What independence means here, stated precisely

v0.4.8's EXP-042–046 were produced by one implementation, `v0.4.8/research-common.js`, which discovers faces by scanning forward from the `[4,8,2,S]` precursor array. This experiment re-derives the same claims through a different path and adds a control v0.4.8 does not contain.

**Independent:**
- **Face discovery.** `parser/v0.1` locates faces by scanning for the gap marker `[12,100,2,vertexCount]` — a different strategy from the forward precursor scan. Agreement on the face set is therefore a real cross-check, not a restatement.
- **Block3 offsets.** Computed in this script from `parser/v0.1`'s own `block2Start` and `secCount` (`block2Start + 16 + 4*secCount`). `parser/v0.1` has no concept of Block3 and never reads it, so this does not inherit v0.4.8's offset arithmetic.
- **Strip triangulation, edge enumeration, face-level edge-incidence classification, and the STL reader** are all written in this file.

**Not independent:**
- The **openswx container decompressor is shared**. A container-level decoding error would affect both paths identically. v0.4.8 flags the same limitation for itself; it is not resolved here.

total edge tokens            112,047

**Zero exceptions in either direction**, matching EXP-043 exactly.

*Note on derivation order:* this edge ordering was derived here independently, from the `2L-3` edge count of an `L`-vertex strip, before v0.4.8's write-up was available to read. The two derivations agree. Recorded because independent convergence on the ordering is stronger evidence than either derivation alone.

## 4. Part 3 — Edge-order falsification control (new; not in v0.4.8)

## 6. Part 5 — Correction: EXP-042's STL residuals are partly a defective export, not tessellation

`v0.4.8/README.md` attributes STL comparison mismatches to tessellation: *"Different STL tessellation remains visible and is archived; curved-model triangle equality is not claimed."*

**That explanation cannot apply to C09.** C09 is a chamfered cube — entirely planar, no curved surface anywhere — yet `EXP042_RESULTS.json` records `unmatchedGenerated: 2, unmatchedReference: 0` for it.

Auditing every controlled `model.STL` by facet-normal group:

| model | STL triangles | axis-aligned face groups |
|---|---|---|
| C00, C01, C02 | 12 | complete |
| **C03_cube_fillet_1mm** | 50 | **−X missing** |
| C04, C05, C06, C07, C08 | 164–332 | complete |
| **C09_cube_chamfer_1mm** | 14 | **−X missing** |
| C10_cube_shell_1mm | 28 | complete |
| **C11_cube_hole_4mm** | 162 | **−X missing** |
| C12_cube_fillet_1mm_edge2 | 52 | complete |

**C03, C09 and C11's STL exports each contain no `-1,0,0` facet-normal group at all** — the −X face is absent from the export. A missing axis-aligned normal group cannot arise from tessellation choice: a differently-tessellated planar face still produces facets with that normal.

Consequences:

1. **C09's residual is fully explained.** It needs 16 triangles; the STL has 14. The two "unmatched generated" triangles are the two 50 mm² halves of the absent −X face (`unmatchedReference: 0` — the strip reading reproduces *every* triangle the STL does contain, and adds the ones it lacks). Tessellation plays no part.
2. **For C03 and C11**, 2 of the unmatched generated triangles are this missing face; the remainder is genuine tessellation difference.
3. **The SLDPRT display mesh is more complete than the STL export**, not less. This sits well with EXP-046's own finding that all 13 controlled SLDPRT meshes are closed under exact triangle-edge matching: the meshes are closed, three of the exports are not.
4. **C03, C09 and C11's STL must not be used as watertight ground truth** for parser validation without accounting for the missing face.

This strengthens rather than weakens v0.4.8's conclusions — the residual it conservatively left unexplained turns out not to be a defect in the strip reading.

## 7. Known gaps

1. **Shared decompressor** — see §1; pipeline independence is partial, not total.
2. **Corpus** is the same 21 modern files, not an independent holdout. This replicates v0.4.8's result on v0.4.8's corpus; it does not extend the claim to other file versions.
3. **No semantic claim.** Nothing here touches Block3's meaning, surface-tag semantics, or scalar-array purpose.
4. **Nothing promoted.** INV-020/021/022 keep the status and scope v0.4.8 gave them; this experiment corroborates them, it does not upgrade them.
5. **Cause of the missing −X faces is unknown** — whether an exporter bug, an export setting, or something about how those three models were generated was not investigated. Only the fact of the omission is established.
