## Phase A: Parametric Differentials (EXP-A through EXP-D)

### EXP-A: Scale (C00 ↔ C01)

**Intended geometric change:** Uniform scale from 10mm to 20mm cube.

**Binary diff results:**
- DL sizes: identical (12,050 bytes each)
- Changed bytes: 355 / 12,050 (2.95%)
- Changed ranges: 182

**Structural comparison:**
- Face count: 6 ↔ 6 (identical)
- All 6 faces: ec=4, vc=4, b1Len=6, secCount=1 (identical structure)
- No structural differences

**Vertex comparison:**
- Max vertex delta: 0.010000 (exactly 10mm = scale factor)
- Changed components: 36/72 (50%)
- All vertex positions scaled by factor of 2.0

**Interpretation:** Scale changes are encoded as Float32 vertex position updates. The topology (face count, edge count, vertex count, section count) is completely invariant to scale. The byte-level change is minimal (2.95%) because only vertex positions change, and the change is concentrated in the position data area.

**Key finding:** Scale is a pure position-data transformation. No structural metadata changes.

### EXP-C: Hole Diameter (C04 ↔ C05)

**Intended geometric change:** Hole diameter from 5mm to 3mm (same position).

**Binary diff results:**
- DL sizes: 19,036 ↔ 17,688 bytes (different!)
- Changed bytes: 6,853 / max(19,036, 17,688) (36.00%)
- Changed ranges: 1,219

**Structural comparison:**
- Face count: 7 ↔ 7 (identical)
- Faces 0-3 (cube faces): ec=4, vc=4 (identical)
- Face 4 (top with hole): ec=7, vc=62 ↔ ec=7, vc=51 (**different vc**)
- Face 5 (bottom with hole): ec=7, vc=64 ↔ ec=7, vc=49 (**different vc**)

- 3 faces with structural differences

**Vertex comparison:**
- Only faces 0-3 compared (matching structure): max delta = 0.000000
- Faces 4-6 have different vertex counts, cannot compare directly

**Interpretation:** Hole diameter changes affect:
1. Vertex positions on the hole boundary (circular approximation with different radius)
2. Vertex counts on the top/bottom faces (more vertices needed for larger circle)
3. Edge/vertex counts on the cylindrical surface (circumference scales with diameter)
4. DL size changes (different total vertex data)

**Key finding:** Hole diameter is a geometrically complex change that affects both vertex positions AND structural metadata (vc, ec, DL size). The cylindrical surface vertex count scales approximately linearly with diameter (70 for 5mm, 56 for 3mm).

## Phase B: Feature Differentials (EXP-E through EXP-I)

### EXP-E: Fillet (C00 ↔ C03)

**Intended geometric change:** 1mm fillet on one edge.

**Binary diff results:**
- DL sizes: 12,050 ↔ 14,350 bytes (different!)
- Changed bytes: 7,477 / max(12,050, 14,350) (52.10%)
- Changed ranges: 1,623

**Structural comparison:**
- Face count: 6 ↔ 7 (different!)
- C00 faces 0-3: ec=4, vc=4 (unchanged cube faces)
- C03 faces 0-1: ec=4, vc=4 (unchanged)
- C03 face 2: ec=4, vc=4 (**modified** — adjacent to fillet)
- C03 face 3: ec=4, vc=4 (**modified** — adjacent to fillet)
- C03 face 4: ec=5, vc=5 (**new face** — top with chamfered corner)
- C03 face 5: ec=5, vc=5 (**new face** — bottom with chamfered corner)
- C03 face 6: ec=13, vc=16 (**new face** — fillet surface)
- 3 faces with structural differences

**Vertex comparison:**
- C00 faces 0-1 ↔ C03 faces 0-1: max delta = 0.000000 (identical)
- C00 face 2 ↔ C03 face 2: max delta = 0.001000 (modified — corner trimmed)
- C00 face 3 ↔ C03 face 3: max delta = 0.001000 (modified — corner trimmed)
- C03 faces 4-6: no corresponding C00 faces

**Interpretation:** Fillet operation:
1. Adds 1 new face (fillet surface, ec=13, vc=16)
2. Modifies 2 existing faces (adjacent faces get corner trimmed)
3. The adjacent faces retain ec=4, vc=4 but vertex positions change
4. The fillet surface has high ec/vc due to circular approximation

**Key finding:** Fillet is a topologically additive operation (+1 face) that modifies 2 adjacent faces. The fillet surface has significantly higher ec/vc than the base cube faces.

## Key Findings

### 1. Position Data is Float32
Vertex positions are stored as IEEE 754 Float32 values in the position data area. Scale and translation changes are encoded as vertex position updates with no structural metadata changes.

### 2. Topology Changes Are Structural
Feature operations (fillet, chamfer, hole, shell) change face counts and vertex/edge counts. The DL size changes proportionally to the number of new faces and vertices.

### 3. Hole Diameter Affects Cylindrical Surface vc
The cylindrical surface vertex count scales approximately linearly with diameter: vc ≈ 14 * diameter_mm (70 for 5mm, 56 for 3mm).

### 4. Hole Position Affects Adjacent Faces
Moving a hole changes vertex counts on the top/bottom faces (which contain the hole boundary) but NOT on the cylindrical surface (which only depends on diameter).

### 5. Feature Operations Are Localized
Modifying one feature (e.g., one hole in a two-hole model) only affects the faces associated with that feature. Unrelated faces remain completely unchanged.

### 6. Curved Surfaces Have High ec/vc
Fillet and cylindrical hole surfaces have significantly higher ec/vc than flat cube faces due to circular approximation. Chamfer (flat) has low ec/vc.

- The relationship between vertex positions and the underlying B-rep parameterization is not established.
