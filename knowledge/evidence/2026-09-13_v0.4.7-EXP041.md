## 1. Method and reuse

Parsing uses `parser/v0.1/src/parser-core.js` read-only and unmodified — the same route EXP-038
used for C12, the only model that previously had a real file.

Per the binding methodological constraints in `RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md`, no new adjacency or
correspondence logic was written:

- **Adjacency**: `computeAdjacency` / `sharedVertexCount` from
  `exp037_edge_location_and_adjacency.js` — real vertex sharing, ≥2 coincident vertices = shares an
  edge. The orientation-label heuristic from `exp033_feature_state.js` is not used.
- **Correspondence**: `matchFaces` from `exp038_nq028_c12_second_edge.js` — axis-aligned
  bounding-box centre, `CENTROID_THRESHOLD = 0.002`, `AMBIGUITY_MARGIN = 0.0005`, with explicit
  ambiguity flagging rather than forced matching. EXP-037's vertex-average centroid is not used.
- **"Directly modified"**: `facesIdentical` — full per-vertex coordinate comparison at tolerance
  1e-6, *not* the ec/vc/secCount/b1Len criterion FH-030 was originally written against.

Face correspondence never uses Block1/Block2 token equality or similarity, which is the subject
under investigation.

## 4. Part 3 — New results from the five previously-unanalyzed models

### 4.1 C04 → C06 (hole position only): a face whose every vertex moved, tokens byte-identical

| face | vc | own vertices | Block1 tokens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 (side walls) | 4→4 | unchanged | unchanged |
| 4 (+Z) | 62→66 | changed | changed (length-forced by INV-016) |
| 5 (−Z) | 64→62 | changed | changed (length-forced by INV-016) |
| **6 (cylinder)** | **70→70** | **all 70 moved** | **byte-identical** |

The cylinder's 70 vertices carry a **single uniform delta, `[0.002, 0, 0]` exactly** (one distinct
delta across all 70 vertices — matching the hole centre moving (5,5)→(7,5)). `vc`, `secCount`, and
`b1Len` (138) are unchanged, so both token arrays have equal length and are comparable
element-wise. `b2Body` is also identical.

**Observation.** First case in the corpus of a face whose own vertex coordinates all changed while
its tokens did not, *inside a model pair where other faces did change*.

**Interpretation.** The existing evidence that raw vertex modification is not sufficient for a token
change (H1a; `RESEARCH_DESIGN_next_experiment.md` §0.1) rested entirely on C01/C02 — whole-model
similarity transforms. Those two pairs produce **zero matched correspondences** under `matchFaces`
(every bbox centre moves far past the 0.002 m threshold), so they never appear in a cross-tabulation
at all and the §0.1 figure had to be assembled by hand from EXP-034. C06 supplies the same
conclusion from a **local** change inside a non-similarity model edit, and it appears directly in
the table.

This does **not** falsify H1b — a pure translation is a similarity transform of the face itself, so
"non-similarity change to the face's own shape" is untouched. What it sharpens is H4: within one
model pair, one face translates with unchanged tokens while two others change. The token change
behaves as a per-face property, not a model-global one.

Consistent with EXP-034 and with the Candidate-E forecast in
`RESEARCH_DESIGN_next_experiment.md` §4 ("essentially rigid translation, which EXP-034 already
predicts leaves tokens unchanged"). That forecast is now measured rather than inferred.

### 4.2 C07 → C08 (second hole only, 5 mm → 3 mm): strongest H4 control to date

Face count 8→8; no faces added or removed.

| face | what it is | vertices | Block1 tokens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | side walls | identical | **identical** |
| 4 | +Z, two inner loops | changed (122→109) | changed |
| 5 | −Z, two inner loops | changed (116→111) | changed |
| **6** | **first hole's cylinder, centre (3,3) — untouched by the edit** | **identical** | **identical** |
| 7 | second hole's cylinder, centre (7,7) — the edited one | changed (70→56) | changed |

Face centres confirming identity of faces 6 and 7 (mm): face6 `[3.00, 3.00, 5.00]`, face7
`[7.00, 7.00, 5.00]`. Face 6 shares **35 vertices with face 4 and 35 with face 5**, both of which
undergo large token changes.

**Observation.** Five of eight faces come through a real feature edit byte-identical in both
geometry and Block1 tokens.

**Interpretation.** Per EXP-028 the DisplayList is re-serialized wholesale on any face change, yet
these five faces are untouched — a global/serialization-state effect (H4) would not leave them so.
H4 is further weakened, and for the first time on a pair with a genuine **non-similarity** feature
edit (C01/C02, the only prior evidence of this kind, are similarity transforms).

It also extends EXP-039's R2 relation — "one-hop propagation from an already-modified neighbour does
not occur", previously 12/12 unchanged — to the **highest-contact case in the corpus**: 35 shared
vertices with each of two heavily-changed neighbours, still no change.

### 4.3 C04 → C11 (diameter 5→4 mm)

Behaves exactly as C04→C05: walls unchanged; faces 4, 5, 6 all change with `b1Len` forced by
INV-016 (vc 62→59, 64→57, 70→64). Length-forced, therefore low-information for the primary
question, exactly as `RESEARCH_DESIGN_next_experiment.md` §2/H5 predicted for the diameter series.

### 4.4 C04 → C07 (one hole → two holes)

`matchFaces` reports `removed=[6], added=[6,7]` — it correctly declines to force-match C04's
centre-(5,5) cylinder to either of C07's (3,3)/(7,7) cylinders. This is the honest output; the
holes genuinely moved. Faces 4/5 change and are adjacent to both new cylinders; walls 0–3 unchanged.

different row sets. Anyone re-deriving these numbers should note the distinction.

## 6. Known gaps

1. **The empty cell is still empty.** H1b and H2 remain inseparable. Nothing in this experiment
   resolves the confound; it only proves the confound survives the corpus expansion.
2. **Correspondence threshold ran to its limit.** The C06 cylinder matched at
   `centroidDist = 0.0019999998616 m` against `CENTROID_THRESHOLD = 0.002 m` — a margin of
   ~1.4 × 10⁻¹⁰ m. The match is genuine (independently confirmed by the uniform `[0.002,0,0]` delta
   and identical `vc`), but it passed by luck. **Any feature translated further than 2 mm will fail
   correspondence and be reported as removed+added**, as C04→C07 already is. Do not retune the
   threshold to force matches; a principled revision would need re-validation across all archived
   pairs, as EXP-038 did.
3. **Not code-independent.** §3's invariant re-verification reuses `parser/v0.1` extraction.
4. **No semantic claim.** No meaning is assigned to any Block1/Block2 token value.
5. **No hypothesis promoted.** Nothing from v0.4.7, including this experiment, has been promoted to
   `KNOWN_INVARIANTS.md`.
6. **STEP/STL files unused.** The corpus ships `model.step` and `model.STL` alongside each
   `model.SLDPRT`. This experiment used only the SLDPRT files. Cross-validating SLDPRT tessellation
   against the STEP B-rep across the full corpus (EXP-028 did this for a small sample and found
   3–12.5% exact vertex match) is now possible corpus-wide and is not done here.
