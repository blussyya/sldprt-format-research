Black lines are Block1 annotations, not a wireframe. They are exactly the strip edges whose stored token is nonzero — INV-021's face-boundary edges. A wrong edge classification would decorate face interiors rather than their outlines.

Scope. This is the display mesh, not the CAD surfaces. Curved faces show the tessellation SolidWorks stored for its own viewport, so facet banding on a cylinder is the data, not a defect. Coordinates are float32, so a 10 mm cube measures 9.9999998 mm. Extents above are the real bounding box in millimetres.

Geometry generated by viewer/export-mesh-data.js from parser/v0.2. Rendered with hand-written WebGL and no external library, matching the project's own no-dependency rule.
